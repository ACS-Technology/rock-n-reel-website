About Rock ’N Reel

It started with a question. Could an electric guitar double as a fishing rig?

Darren started building Rock ’N Reel in Wisconsin, using a Strat-style guitar, a spincast reel and a growing pile of printed parts. The goal was to keep the guitar playable and give the reel a proper mount and a path for the fishing line.

That means a lot of small decisions about where the line runs, how the housing opens and how the whole thing sits on the guitar. The Garage is where those decisions and the builds that led to them get written down.
