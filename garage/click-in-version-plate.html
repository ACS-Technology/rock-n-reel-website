A click-in version plate

A bench note on giving the prototype’s version label a better home.

What prompted it

On the V3.3 print, the version plate felt flimsy where it hung below the enclosure.

What to try

Make a separate plate that clicks into the opposite side of the enclosure, taking a cue from the badge plate. The removable face could also take a printed version sticker.

What to check on the bench

Check that the plate installs and removes cleanly, stays put while handling the enclosure and has enough support at the edges. Record the fit after a test print.
