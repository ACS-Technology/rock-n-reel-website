The reel foot had other plans

The reel foot in the STL looked like a shortcut. The physical foot sent us back to the calipers.

What went sideways

The existing model was a poor reference for the shape under the rear ledge. Measuring the real part gave us a better starting point for checking the pocket width and the clearance below the ledge.

What comes next

Check the corrected pocket against the physical foot and verify that it can slide under the ledge and seat fully on a test print. Fit is still unverified.
