V0 · Draft build log

The idea

A guitar that could fish.

The starting point

The idea was to keep the electric guitar playable while giving it a spincast reel and a way to guide fishing line along the instrument. That set the basic layout for the first physical build.

What needed figuring out

The reel needed a mounting point that would leave the strings and controls accessible. The line also needed a path from the reel to a guide at the headstock.
