A tighter enclosure

Switching to the Zebco 202 and revisiting how the housing fits together.

The redesign

The Zebco 202 gave the enclosure a new shape to work around. The housing design moved toward a slimmer profile, with captive nuts and machine screws for removable assembly.

Fit notes

The V3.2 print surfaced several things to revisit, including the reel-foot pocket, captive-nut fit and the front PEX connector. The curved front changed the fit of the connector carried over from the earlier design.
